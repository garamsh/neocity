# Survival Rules

The pure C++ progression state of the monster-mouth escape game and the ownership boundaries of its later features.

## Current decisions

- The `survival` static library in `src/survival/` depends only on the C++ standard library. It does not link with Godot. Its public boundary is [`include/survival/simulation.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/simulation.hpp), [`include/survival/world.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/world.hpp), [`include/survival/exploration.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/exploration.hpp), [`include/survival/workshop.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/workshop.hpp), [`include/survival/escape.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/escape.hpp), [`include/survival/companion.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/companion.hpp), and [`include/survival/content.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/content.hpp), and internal implementation and unit tests sit in the same library's `src/`.
- [`Simulation`](../../src/survival/include/survival/simulation.hpp) owns the two actors' location and inventory, plus the day count, phase, shared remaining exploration time, world time, acid, and run seed. It offers value snapshots and explicit commands for entering, returning, collecting ingredients, advancing time, consuming ingredients, finishing the day, and escaping. The base is part of the untimed workshop phase. An actor has no health, stamina, or carried tool, as [ADR 0026](adr/0026-remove-health-stamina-carried-tools-and-passage-repair.md) decides.
- Exploration time passes only while at least one actor is exploring. On expiry both return to base and the phase changes to the workshop phase. A voluntary return sends only that actor home, preserving the day count, inventories, shared remaining time, and acid. Switching to the workshop phase and stopping time when both are at base is a temporary boundary of the current foundation and does not settle the final base time rule.
- With time remaining, an actor can re-enter after returning.
- The caller supplies a positive exploration budget and a seed. Progress is independent of frame partitioning when the same command order and total exploration time are passed directly to the shared `Simulation`. Time is passed as integer microseconds rather than wall-clock time, and negative time is refused with no state change. This guarantee does not extend to exploration return times that follow from world changes.
- The seed is a run-identifying input preserved in the snapshot, and `Workshop` reads it to initialize its own random state. `Simulation` consumes no random values. The Godot host connection and the screen are in [session](session.md) and [presentation](presentation.md).
- Tests are in [`src/simulation_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/simulation_test.cpp), [`src/world_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/world_test.cpp), [`src/exploration_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/exploration_test.cpp), [`src/workshop_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/workshop_test.cpp), [`src/escape_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/escape_test.cpp), [`src/companion_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/companion_test.cpp), and [`src/content_test.cpp`](../../src/survival/src/content_test.cpp), registered in the same `survival_test`. Checks that directly compose world, progression, exploration, and workshop together are library integration tests and do not verify the real screen connection. `project/e2e/survival.gd`, `project/e2e/escape.gd`, `project/e2e/loss.gd`, and `project/tests/survival_timing.gd` verify that connection.

### World state

- `World` distinguishes NPCs, terrain, buildings, ladders, entrances, and material sources by identifier. The caller supplies each object's integer world area, damage and exposure thresholds, and material source owner and supplied ingredient. A material source supplies exactly one ingredient. It embeds no final map or tuning values. IDs persist through destruction and exposure, and value snapshots give state, traversability, and supply availability. The traversable value applies to terrain and passages and does not imply collision checks of any other kind.
- A material source can supply when it is itself intact and, if an owner is configured, that NPC, building, or terrain is also intact. An owner not yet visible does not supply. Exploration validates acquisition and stock use.
- Progression input is the run's day. `World::update(day)` refuses a day lower than the last one it saw, and an identical day does not transition state again. Acid no longer reaches the world: it only rises and loses the run. The return-to-base command neither owns nor resets the world.
- Damage follows the day alone, as [ADR 0027](adr/0027-fix-the-street-and-aquarium-maps-and-damage-them-by-day.md) decides: an object is exposed from `exposed_from_day` and destroyed from `destroyed_from_day`, and `damage_stage(day)` names the stage, `Standing` on days 1 to 4, `Cracked` on days 5 to 9 and `Ruined` from day 10. A damaged passage stays damaged for the rest of the run, since days only increase. The day changes only at base, so the map never changes under an actor.
- Exposure persists once its day is reached. Each stage changes cells or structures on every map: a building falls and another passage or source appears. Debris physics and chained collapse between buildings are not implemented.
- World time remains the run's clock, held by `Simulation`: the exploration time spent today plus a full budget for every finished day, spent or not. It no longer feeds the world. `Exploration` updates `World` with the simulation's day on every advance, so a finished day reaches the world on the next advance, at base too. The caller does not separately call `World`'s update in the same frame. `Exploration.advance` takes no acid. The host composes these concrete features at the existing single composition site.

### Exploration behavior

- `Exploration` does not own the `Simulation` and `World` it receives. It owns both actors' detailed positions and the shared material source stock, and applies entry, return, and collection through the existing progression commands. Actor position and time commands during a run go through exploration.
- The city is seen from the side, as [ADR 0007](adr/0007-move-actors-through-a-side-view-with-gravity.md) decides. Each actor's feet are a fixed-point position, `SUBCELLS` (32) units to a cell, with a stance: standing, in the air, or climbing. `CellMap` rasterises a world snapshot into cells once per movement unit: intact terrain is a one-row floor, an intact ladder is climbable, an intact building blocks unless an intact entrance or ladder overlaps it, and a broken entrance blocks. A broken ladder only stops being climbable, so world object order does not change the result. A floor is stood on and jumped up through, and the top of a blocking cell is stood on too, so a one-cell rubble heap is a hurdle to jump.
- `physics_tick` moves a body one 20ms step by the integer speeds in `PHYSICS` and reports whether it ran. Walking keeps to a floor and walks a one-row step up or down, which is all stairs and ramps are; off an edge the body falls. Gravity adds to the fall speed up to a cap, and a body lands on the first floor line below it. A jump from a floor or a ladder starts at a fixed upward speed, so it clears a gap of two cells at the same height or one cell a row up, and stops rising under a blocking cell. Up or down on a ladder climbs it, and walking off a ladder steps onto the floor of its cell, if any. A body that drops below every object has fallen into the acid. These rules know nothing of the screen: the camera that follows the humans over the map is presentation's, as [ADR 0009](adr/0009-follow-the-humans-with-a-close-camera.md) decides.
- Movement is in 100ms units of five physics steps each, and the leftover movement time of short frames is preserved per actor. Actual exploration time is consumed immediately on every call. Both actors' inputs are taken together, the shared time is consumed once, and movement after expiry is forbidden. The world changes only when the day does, and the day changes only at base, so no damage happens during exploration and no result varies with how calls are partitioned. The Godot host delivers one constant 100ms unit.
- A collection records its ingredient at once and holds the collecting actor for `ExplorationConfig::collection_time`, the same for a human and AI, as [ADR 0008](adr/0008-hold-the-collecting-actor-instead-of-the-shared-clock.md) decides. The shared clock runs on for everyone, so both actors can collect at once and the partner keeps moving. A held actor ignores movement and is refused another collection and a return with `Busy`; its hold runs down with the exploration time that passes and is shown in the snapshot. Expiry and a fall into the acid end the hold, and the ingredient is kept. A refused collection holds no one, and a return gives no time back.
- Walking moves 25 units, under a cell, per movement unit and running 50, only on a floor; climbing moves 20 and a jump rises about 1.4 rows. Running has no limit and never tires. The speeds are temporary tuning values. Interaction distance, start point, and stock are caller inputs and all temporary tuning values. The two roles have no difference in ability.
- Vending machine, NPC, shop, and bag markers all connect to the world's material source IDs. The ingredient collection command applies after checking that the material source exists and its access distance, supply state, and remaining shared stock. Spent stock is not restored by returning or setting out again.
- A manual return, a fall into the acid, or expiry sets the detailed position to base, standing. Setting out again starts at the designated city entrance and does not reuse earlier partial movement. Collection requests and holds are not retained, so an earlier request does not run on the next departure. Inventories, the world, and shared time are preserved under the existing progression rules. Two actors travel between maps together: an `Exploration` holds `MapExit` records, a zone on one map and an arrival cell on another, and after each movement unit the map changes only when both actors stand in the same exit zone, both then standing at the arrival cell. One actor in the zone changes nothing. `enter` is refused while the other actor is in the city off the street, `collect` refuses a source on another map with `OutOfRange`, and a party entirely at base is on the street again. The snapshot carries the current map and the building each actor is inside (`Interior`).

### Workshop work

- `Workshop` does not own the `Simulation` it receives. It owns role checks, identified ingredients, stored food, the history of fed foods and ingredients, the last day's result, and its own random state. `Simulation` handles atomic ingredient consumption, acid, the next-day transition, and exploration budget restoration. This boundary follows the [PM decision](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/26#issuecomment-6553559757).
- All workshop commands apply only in the workshop phase with both actors at base. Workshop commands use no exploration time and have no time limit.
- Only the appraiser appraises and only the chef cooks. Feeding and ending the day unfed are not tied to a role. An appraisal result distinguishes only success or failure and the identified ingredient, and whether failure is misinformation or a failure indication is not decided.
- Appraisal and cooking both succeed with a fixed 90%, the [user decision](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21#issuecomment-6526232827)'s intact-tool value kept as the only value after [ADR 0026](adr/0026-remove-health-stamina-carried-tools-and-passage-repair.md) removed the damaged 40% and the tools that lowered it.
- `Simulation`'s finish-day raises acid, moves to the next day, and returns the exploration budget to its configured value. Acid stops at full. Feeding or ending the day unfed finishes the day in one command, so there is one feeding per day. The world and stock are not reset by the day.
- The last day's result is the finished day, the monster's reaction, the score, the acid rise, and on a fed day the dish with its score part by part. `dish_score` computes those parts from a dish and the eating history, for feeding and for the screen's forecast alike, and reads no `Recipe::disliked`.

#### Randomness

- The owner is `Workshop`. Appraisal and cooking share one `std::mt19937` initialized once at construction from the run seed.
- Consumption order is command call order. One actually attempted appraisal or cooking consumes one value, and the snapshot's `rolls` shows the cumulative consumption count. Cooking rolls after consuming ingredients. Invalid role, phase, and ingredient requests consume neither state nor random values.
- The roll is the engine output modulo 100, and it succeeds below 90. A cook's same roll also decides the dish: the lower half of the success range is fine and the upper half plain, so no extra value is consumed. Only the engine output fixed by the standard is used, and standard distributions, whose results differ by implementation, are not. The bias from `2^32` not being a multiple of 100 is at most 96 in `2^32`.
- There is no general random service. `Escape` owns a second stream, described in [Escape](#escape). Consumption in one stream never moves the other.

#### Temporary policies

The following are temporary values that make the prototype playable and are not final balance confirmed by the user.

- Recipes, disliked food markers, new food and new ingredient scores, the satisfaction threshold, and acid rises are supplied by the caller and have no default. Flavors and the fine dish score default to adding nothing.
- The score adds the first-eaten food score, the score for each first-eaten ingredient kind, each ingredient's taste, and the fine dish score for a fine dish. Novelty and taste are added, so that the threshold can demand both: the [user decision](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21#issuecomment-6566139712) is that new and delicious food holds the acid, and that taste depends on the ingredients and the cooking result.
- At or above the threshold acid does not rise. Below it acid rises by the configured amount scaled to the shortfall, rounded up, so a bland repeat costs more than a delicious one and any meal below the threshold raises acid. A disliked food raises acid by its configured amount regardless of score, and `WorkshopSnapshot::rejected_foods` remembers it. No feeding lowers acid.
- Ending the day without feeding is a [temporary PM decision](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/26#issuecomment-6553559757) that keeps the date from being blocked by a lack of ingredients. The acid penalty is a call input, and the command creates no ingredients or food.
- Cooking needs every ingredient of the recipe to be identified. Identification is per ingredient kind and lasts the whole run. Retries after a failed appraisal are not limited. Re-appraising an already identified ingredient is refused.
- A failed cook consumes the ingredients and makes no food. Food is stored until fed and does not spoil with the date.
- At base the two actors' inventories are used together, and the chef's are consumed first. In the workshop phase the day can end even with exploration time remaining, and the remaining time does not carry over: it passes on the world clock.

### Escape

- `Escape` does not own the `Simulation` it receives. It owns the daily check and its own `std::mt19937`, seeded once at construction from the run seed and a fixed stream constant through `std::seed_seq`, whose output the standard fixes. Building an engine from the run seed alone would repeat the workshop rolls, so that form is not used.
- A check applies in the workshop phase, rolls once for the latest finished day, and consumes one value. A repeat call on the same day draws nothing, so the host checks after every command. A roll waits while the party is in the city.
- A hit calls `Simulation::escape`, which applies only in the workshop phase.

#### Temporary escape values

These make the ending reachable and are not final balance confirmed by the user.

- The first check follows the finishing of day 30, so day 30 counts. Each later finished day is checked.
- A check hits at 20%, a flat chance that does not grow. The values are `TEMPORARY_ESCAPE` in `escape.hpp`.
- A hit is taken at once: the player does not accept the chance, and the party escapes wherever the day ended. What causes the escape is not depicted.

### Loss

- Acid reaching `Config::full_acid` loses the run, a [user decision](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21#issuecomment-6566136667). The value is a rule, and the screen reads it. `Simulation`'s finish-day sets `Phase::Lost` inside the same command that raises acid, on the day counter's new day, so nothing can act between the two. A loss takes no inventory.
- **Escape and loss on one day:** the loss comes first. Finishing the day decides the loss, and `Escape::check` then finds the run over, reports `NotDue`, and draws nothing, so a day that fills the acid cannot escape on that day's check.
- After either end of the run no command of `Simulation`, `Exploration`, `Workshop`, or `Escape` changes any state: `enter` and `Exploration::advance` answer `RunOver`, and the other commands keep their existing refusals. `SurvivalRun` runs no further step. `World` has no phase: a caller holding it directly can still call `update`, and play reaches it only through `Exploration`.

### Companion and control

- `Companion` holds no game state and no random state. It reads `GameView`, a value copy of the four snapshots, and the `ControlMap`, and returns the same `ActorCommand` for the same input. A human's presses and AI's decisions leave as one command type: movement goes to `Exploration::advance`, and an action goes through `apply` to the owning feature, which keeps every refusal and rule. `Companion` updates nothing itself.
- AI reads only what a player has: the snapshots and the recipes of the caller's configuration. It does not read `Recipe::disliked`, since a dislike is learned by feeding, and it never observes `Workshop`'s random state.
- `ControlMap` holds one `Controller` per actor. One-player play makes the chosen actor human and the other AI; two-player play makes both human.
- Switching and leaving are separate local commands. Switching trades the two controllers, so a lone human takes the other actor and two humans stay as they are. Leaving hands only that actor to AI. Both keep the actor's position and inventory, because AI continues from the snapshots. The caller owns the `ControlMap`, and the host keeps one human in it, as [session](session.md) records.
- [`src/input/survival.cpp`](../../src/input/survival.cpp) samples Godot actions into `PlayerInput`, bound in `project.godot`.
- Two local key sets exist because two humans share one computer; the two input sets also let a local check drive both actors. A lone human holds the first set on either actor, so switching needs no rebinding. Two humans hold the first set on the chef and the second on the appraiser. The action names and every binding are temporary.
- The caller reads a fresh view for each actor after applying the other's action, and advances at most one 100ms movement tick between commands, because a movement command is valid for one unit.
- `Exploration` and route planning share `CellMap`, `physics_tick`, and area distance in `exploration.hpp`, so a route cannot disagree with movement. `links` finds the moves from a standing or climbing cell by running the physics from it: walk or jump left or right, climb up or down, each to the cell where the body comes to rest, and a jump only where walking does not reach the next column. A link that would end in the acid is no link. `steer` turns a move into the input that walks it; a jump waits until the body is near the edge, and only a take-off that lands on the same row from either end of that reach is a link. Each command searches reachable cells and runs the physics for each link, which suits the current maps. Movement and route planning take a per-map `CellMap` and `links` (`on_map`).

#### Temporary AI policies

The following are the minimum that keeps a game from blocking, not final behavior or tactics.

- A collecting actor's command is empty, under a human or AI, until its hold is over.
- AI walks, climbs, and jumps but never runs, and collects from every available source with stock: what the party carries none of first, then the nearest. In the air it holds the way it went until it lands. `Companion::errand` names that target and the first move towards it, so a screen can tell a human the same way; for an actor in the air it plans from where a straight drop lands, so the line holds through a jump or a fall.
- AI sets out only for a source it can still reach: `Companion::worth_entering` tells whether any stocked source stands within reach of the entrance over the passages still standing, so a party whose stock lies behind a broken passage stays at base instead of setting out for nothing. A passage never returns, so the answer only shrinks as days pass. The AI stays on the street; joint travel for the AI is [#225](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/225)'s.
- AI with a human partner follows that partner: it sets out only while the partner is in the city and returns when the partner is at base, because workshop commands need both actors at base. With an AI partner, each sets out while time remains and something is reachable from the entrance.
- AI in the city with nothing reachable returns to base, and it enters only when something is reachable from the entrance. Expiry returns it otherwise. Waiting at base therefore never holds the day open.
- The appraiser appraises every unidentified carried ingredient and retries a failed roll under the existing unlimited retry policy. The chef cooks one dish when no stored dish can be fed: an uneaten recipe before a repeat, the tastiest first within each, and never a rejected food. It reads a dislike only from `rejected_foods`, so it can feed a disliked recipe once.
- A human in the party owns setting out and ending the day, since AI acting first in an untimed workshop would take the day from them. In an AI-only party the chef ends the day after base work and exploration are done: it feeds an uneaten dish first, then any other stored dish, never a rejected food, and otherwise ends the day unfed at the caller's `unfed_penalty`. Ending the day belongs to one actor so that one step cannot end two days.
- A human's `interact` picks the first eligible target by the same selection, and the screen names that target. A human's `feed` feeds the dish AI would, so it does nothing while only rejected foods are stored. A screen choosing another target builds the `Action` directly, as the host does for a human chef's dish, recorded in [session](session.md). `Companion` lets two humans end the day in one step; the host guards it, as [session](session.md) records.

### Temporary content

- [`content.hpp`](../../src/survival/include/survival/content.hpp) builds one run's maps, ground, decorations, ingredients, recipes, stock, hazard cells, and tuning, with display names. The seed is a run identifier only: the maps and the supply are the same for every seed. Every value is temporary and is not final balance or a final map. Library integration tests in `content_test.cpp` run an AI-only party to day 30 on five seeds and check that no day before the first escape check ends unfed, that collection goes on after day 14, and that the first day ends by time while stock remains. A path check walks every map on every day from 1 to 60 over the same `CellMap` and `links` that movement uses, from the cell a party starts on, and requires every offered source, exit and interior zone to be reached; a second test cuts a passage on each stage and requires the check to name what it strands. Tests also hold two seeds to the same maps and supply, each stage to a change of cells on every map and only on its day, and the maps' sizes, buildings, floors and stairs.
- The budget is 18 seconds a day, a collection time of 1.5 seconds, full acid 100, new food 50, new ingredient 5, satisfying score 100, fine dish 30, disliked rise 15, unsatisfied rise 10 at a score of zero, and unfed penalty 12. Taste per ingredient runs from Pickles 10 to Truffle 55. Every wanted new dish can reach the threshold when cooked fine. A repeat scores its taste alone: only a fine Truffle Roast, Truffle Soup, or Surf and Turf reaches it, and every other repeat falls short.
- The street is 64 by 16 and the aquarium 44 by 16, as [ADR 0027](adr/0027-fix-the-street-and-aquarium-maps-and-damage-them-by-day.md) decides. The street has the base entrance at its west end, the aquarium entrance at its east end (exit zone (62,10,2,2) to the aquarium, arrival cell (4,10)), three enterable buildings with interior zones, the kitchen shop, the noodle shop and the cold store, and floors on three or more rows joined by ladders, stair steps and jumps. The aquarium is a flooded theme building with a lobby by the way back (exit zone (0,9,2,2) to the street, arrival cell (58,11)), an upper and a lower gallery, a stair, ladders, and a skywalk that arrives with the ruined stage. The base is at cell (1,8) and the entry at (5,8). No row holds a run of ground longer than a third of the street's width or half the aquarium's, so every route bends, climbs, jumps, or drops.
- The street's damage stages: on day 5 the roof ladder goes and a fire escape and the drifted ferry arrive, the fire escape carrying the kitchen roof; on day 10 the rotten bridge and the old kiosk go. The aquarium's upper span goes on day 5 and its lower span on day 10, when the skywalk and its ladders appear. Two one-cell rubble heaps are buildings that never fall.
- `Content::ground` names the surface of every terrain cell: road, cracked road, pothole, the left or right end of a broken road, tilted slab, rubble, plank bridge, or buried car. Rubble marks a cell a rubble heap blocks, and every other kind is drawing only. `Content::decorations` places tilted elevated trains, building shells, hanging cables and pipes, dead neon signs, flesh growths, rubble piles, broken elevated pillars, and street lamps by area. `Content::landmarks` names the objects drawn with a picture of their own: the kiosk wreck, the cold store, the drifted ferry, the pier as an iron platform, and the rotten bridge. No rule reads any of the three; the screen draws them.
- Acid drowns low ground and shows it, but destroys nothing and limits no source or bag; its only effect is to lose the run at full acid.
- There are 11 recipes, one disliked, so after ten days the acid holds only on a fine repeat of the tastiest recipes, and the other days spend the gauge by how far they fall short. That is what makes taste and supply matter to the end of the run.
- Supply is spread out in space and time, so a day is spent walking between sources, not collecting at one. From day 2 to day 50 three washed-up bags of 2 land as each day starts, one in each lane: the shallows and the gauge pier, which the shop roof by the kiosk, the gauge ledge and the rope bridge replace from day 19; the kitchen loft; and the east half's roof, loft, walkways and pier. Each lane cycles through its landing cells and its cargo, and from day 19 Meat and Truffle wash up too. The tide takes an uncollected bag back five days after it lands, before its lane comes back to that cell, so no two sources share a cell while both are on the map, and the first day still ends by time while stock remains.
- Sources the acid used to destroy now stand for the whole run: the shallows, the tomato and pickle machines, the lost basket, the noodle vendor, the kitchen shop and the cold store door supply on every day, and the cold locker behind the door stays reachable. Day stages take away only passages and buildings that no source depends on, so the seed moves no supply.
- A bag of any kind, the lost basket included, leaves the map once it is empty.
- A bag follows the day alone, so a party with nothing in reach, or one that never sets out, still sees it land on schedule: three a day from day 2 to day 50, with no acid limit. The ferry and its bag come on day 5, the roof ladder breaks on day 5, and the bridge falls on day 10.
- Thirty dishes for the first escape check take 34 attempts with the cook's 90%, because a burned dish spends its ingredients, and the reachable stock covers that. The collection time, the bag size, the stage days, and every score above are temporary values for the user's final decision.
- Hazard cells are acid drips and have no effect on play: `Content` lists them and the screen draws them, and `Simulation`, `World`, and `Exploration` do not read them.

- Features are added as concrete features inside the same `survival` boundary, with no general event bus, shared engine, or empty feature folder. Not all features accumulate in `simulation.cpp`.
- Exploration uses the world's read-only contract. The PM assigns any contract change needed between them to one owner, and neither includes the other feature's internal state.
- Exploration time is delivered to shared progression exactly once. The exploration elapsed amount other features read is the difference in remaining time before and after progression, so it excludes workshop time and overrun after expiry.
- Feature Workers do not modify shared headers, build files, or architecture documents at the same time. Later feature-specific result types and new shared commands are settled in the actual feature issue, and contract changes are gathered to the integration owner before consumers connect.

## Rationale

- The [game baseline](../design/monster-mouth-escape.md) separates user decisions from temporary runtime behavior and open questions. The [parent #21](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21) and [foundation #23](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/23) set the implementation scope.
- This applies the shallow purpose folders and independent rules boundary of the existing [structure](structure.md) and the purpose-based layout of [ADR 0004](adr/0004-adopt-the-godot-cpp-template-layout.md). [ADR 0006](adr/0006-ship-one-game-behind-a-title-screen.md) makes it the application's only game. [ADR 0007](adr/0007-move-actors-through-a-side-view-with-gravity.md) moves its actors through a side view with gravity, on fixed-point positions and a fixed physics step. [ADR 0008](adr/0008-hold-the-collecting-actor-instead-of-the-shared-clock.md) makes a collection hold its actor instead of spending the shared clock, with the supply spread into small daily bags. [ADR 0019](adr/0019-lay-the-city-out-as-the-first-mockup-twice-over.md) lays the city out as the first mockup's screen, twice over.
- The distinct exploration and base behaviors and the two actors' return-preservation rules are the responsibility of an independent survival library, verified apart from the host.

## Open questions

- The distinction between both actors briefly returning and actual cooking, and the final behavior of the exploration clock during a base stay. Currently both at base is treated as the untimed workshop phase.
- The real exploration budget, acid units, the final rise formula, arrival timing, next-day start conditions, the final movement speeds and jump reach, and later feature-specific result types. Test inputs do not imply balance decisions.
- Whether appraisal failure is misinformation or a failure indication, retry limits, the final result of cooking failure, and the unfed acid penalty and feedings per day.
- The final timing and probability of the daily escape chance, whether it grows, how a player accepts it, and what causes the escape.
- Whether AI should end the day or set out on its own when its human partner stays idle. Currently AI waits for the human.
- Whether a human appraiser can direct an AI chef's dish. Currently the AI chef picks its own.
- The save and restore format. Current snapshots are read-only value passing and not a save file or restore.
