# Monster Mouth Escape

Records the decision baseline for the game of surviving and escaping a city swallowed by a monster through exploration, cooking, and feeding.

## User decisions

The baseline is the [body of parent #21](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21), the [October 30 implementation details](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21#issuecomment-6526232827), and the [exploration, return, and escape record](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21#issuecomment-6544165358) that supplements them. The later [decision to recover 3 slots on return](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/23#issuecomment-6544525723) then applies. This return rule replaces the earlier permanent death concept.

| Subject | Decision |
|---|---|
| Setting and actors | A giant monster swallows a city. The chef and the green appraiser gather ingredients, feed the monster every day to hold back the rising acid, and look for an escape. |
| Mutants | Animals and people the monster swallowed are slowly changed by the radioactive environment inside it into mutants. Mutants patrol set stretches of the city and are a danger to the actors. Source: [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Players | One or two players on one computer. In one-player play the player chooses a controlled actor and AI takes the other. Online play was dropped by the decision on [#21](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21). The AI partner keeps the shared-screen and travel rules below. |
| Exploration | Both actors explore together and have the same role while exploring. The magnifier and spatula are decoration, not exploration abilities. Health, stamina and running that spends stamina are removed by [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Time and return | Exploration has a time limit, and on expiry actors return to base automatically. Cooking has no time limit for now. |
| Hazards and return | Touching a mutant or a fixed obstacle sends that actor away, and the actor reappears 10 seconds later where the companion is. A fall into the acid also sends the actor back to base. If both actors touch one, they return to base. Returning to base loses no inventory, and the day count, remaining exploration time, and map state are not reset. This replaces the earlier 3-slot health and its recovery on return, per [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Maps | Random generation is replaced by fixed maps: one street map, one large building map with a completely different theme, and the base. Ordinary buildings are entered within the same map with a darkening and background-change effect. Only the large themed building is entered as a separate map, through a fade transition. Ladders, stairs and similar structures give the swallowed-city feel. More maps are a later goal. Source: [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Daily damage | As days pass, assets or backgrounds of damaged parts change. Damage can block a path, but a path to the goal always exists, and code checks this by graph search. This replaces destruction of NPCs, terrain and objects by acid and time as the map rule, per [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Ingredients | Ingredients come from vending machines, NPCs, shops, bags, and the like. Repairing broken ladders and entrances with tools is removed by [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Shared screen and travel | Both actors are confined to one screen, as in Metal Slug. Both must be together to move to the next map, and the map layout does not let the two actors be on different maps at once. Source: [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Search and inventory | Pressing the search button near a searchable object opens that object's slots like an inventory, and items are moved from there to the actor's own inventory. There is only a basic inventory, and it is reasonably large. The inventory has a weight, and exceeding it reduces movement speed. This replaces the earlier hold on the bag system, per [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Fridge | The base has a fridge, a store that items are moved into from the inventory. Nothing spoils. Source: [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Base roles | The appraiser appraises and the chef cooks. |
| Tools | Tools, their 90% and 40% success rates, damage, and replacement are removed by [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). The magnifier and spatula stay as decoration. |
| Feeding | The monster wants new foods and ingredients. Repeats score low and can fall below the threshold. A disliked food causes rising acid and map damage. New and delicious food holds the acid back, by stopping the rise and not by lowering it, and how delicious a dish is depends on its ingredients and the cooking result. |
| Loss | [Acid reaching full loses the run](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/21#issuecomment-6566136667). No inventory is lost. |
| AI partner | AI does the same work as a human under the same rules, and its share is not limited. |
| Endings | The code holds several endings. The one implemented in this goal is "the monster is satisfied". Deliberately making the monster vomit and escaping through a map element are later goals. The earlier rule of a daily escape chance after 30 days is replaced by this structure, per [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |
| Screen | The UI follows the initial mockup `search-mockup-v1.png` (commit 241124d5ba2dcef937c904623f1e5e3cb03fbcf5), with an inventory screen of search slots and own slots added. The health and inventory UI at the bottom of the screen is removed. Source: [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). |

Decisions from [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218) are recorded here before they are built. The sections below list what the code implements, and a decision in the table that they do not mention is still to come, not implemented.

## Scope implemented on the foundation

Health, stamina, carried tools, passage repair and base tool damage are removed by [#220](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/220) under [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218). Fixed maps, mutants and obstacles, the shared screen, search and inventory, the fridge and the endings structure are decisions of [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218) not yet implemented; sections below that describe the foundation read with those removals applied.

- [Foundation #23](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/23) provides state and commands for the two actors, the first day, the base and exploration phases, inventories, shared remaining time, initial acid, and the run seed. Time expiry, return, re-entry, and inventory preservation can be executed.
- [Exploration #24](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/24) provides, in pure C++, the two actors' identical detailed movement, collision, and running with no limit, fall returns when a body falls into the acid, and material source access, supply, and shared stock validation.
- The decision that cooking has no time limit is reflected as a boundary where the workshop phase consumes no exploration time.
- Voluntary return and time expiry return actors to base. Returning does not reset inventories, the day count, remaining exploration time, or acid.
- Switching to the workshop phase and stopping the exploration clock when both actors are at base is also a temporary boundary of the current foundation. The decision that cooking has no time limit does not settle the final time rule for every base stay.
- The caller supplies exploration time. The short budgets, ingredient IDs, and seeds in tests are verification inputs, not product tuning values. The world reacts to acid given as a separate input.
- [World #25](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/25) provides, as read-only value snapshots, stable IDs and areas for NPCs, terrain, buildings, passages, and material sources, traversal and supply availability after destruction, and the appearance of unexposed buildings, routes, and material sources.
- The caller supplies world damage and exposure thresholds. Test thresholds and layouts are not final balance or maps. Damage uses the run's world time, the exploration time spent today plus a full day for every finished day whether anyone set out, or an increase in historical peak acid, and an acid drop or a return does not reset the destruction or exposure history.
- Exploration's 100ms movement unit and its speeds are temporary values. Since [#109](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/109) the city is a side view with gravity, a jump, ladders, stairs, and ramps, as [ADR 0007](../architecture/adr/0007-move-actors-through-a-side-view-with-gravity.md) records; the speeds and the jump's reach are temporary. The caller supplies the entrance, interaction distance, and stock. Final physics, speed, and balance are not settled. Short frames also consume exploration time immediately and preserve only the movement remainder.
- Vending machines, NPCs, shops, and bags use the same ingredient collection validation. The two actors cannot consume the same stock twice, and returning or setting out again restores neither stock nor the world.
- Exploration consumes the two actors' time once and passes the run's world time to the world. External acid input applies to the world.
- [Workshop #26](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/26) provides, in pure C++, appraisal, cooking, and feeding, the score difference between new foods and ingredients and repeated foods, acid rises from disliked food and from falling below the threshold, and the next-day transition with exploration budget restoration. Progression's acid is a real value that the host passes to `Exploration`. The day count and day result serve the connection of the escape check after day 30.
- Recipes, scores, thresholds, and acid rises are temporary values the caller supplies. Requiring identified cooking ingredients, spending ingredients on a failed cook, food storage, allowing appraisal retries, no acid fall, and one feeding per day are temporary policies, not final rules. Ending the day without feeding is also a [temporary PM decision](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/26#issuecomment-6553559757); the caller sets the acid penalty, and the screen reports it. Whether appraisal failure is misinformation or a failure indication is not decided.
- [Companion #27](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/27) provides, in pure C++, per-actor human or AI control, the separate local switch and leave commands, and a deterministic AI that moves, collects, appraises, cooks, feeds, and ends the day through the same commands a person uses. An AI-only party keeps the days moving when ingredients are missing or unreachable. A human partner keeps the decisions to set out and end the day.
- Temporary policies, not final rules: AI walks, climbs, and jumps but never runs, collects everything available, cooks one dish at a time, and does not use a dislike it has not learned. Key bindings and the human's first-eligible target selection are also temporary.
- [Screen integration #28](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/28) makes the game playable: the main menu starts it, the player chooses the chef, the appraiser, or two local players, and real input runs exploration, the timed return, appraisal, cooking, feeding, and the next day. The screen shows map damage, stock, and acid. Escape, endings, and cinematics are in the [#43](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/43) entry below.
- Temporary, not settled: the screen is a `_draw` grid over a primitive monster mouth with the ascent sprites, and the concept images remain the art target. The map, recipes, stock, 18-second day, scores, acid rises, and key bindings are temporary content. [Survival](../architecture/survival.md#temporary-content) lists the values.
- [Escape and endings #43](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/43) lets a game run from the main menu to an ending. After day 30 a daily check can end the run, an escaped run takes no further command, and the opening and ending have a playback contract for [#29](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/29) with a placeholder card while no art exists.
- Temporary, not settled: the first check after day 30 finishes, the flat 20% chance, a hit being taken at once, the card text, and the card lengths. [Survival](../architecture/survival.md#temporary-escape-values) lists the values. The cause of the escape and the ending itself are undecided; vomiting stays an example. [#218](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/218) replaces the daily escape chance after day 30 with an endings structure and a "the monster is satisfied" ending, and that replacement is not implemented.
- [Acid loss #62](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/62) ends a run when acid reaches full, with a loss card under the same cinematic contract. On a day that both fills the acid and would roll an escape, the loss comes first. The score adds novelty, ingredient taste, and a fine dish's bonus; a satisfying meal stops the rise, and a meal below the threshold raises acid by its shortfall. Rising acid strands stock left in the sources it destroys.
- Temporary, not settled: the formula and all its values, that half of a cook's success range is fine, a collection holding its actor for 1.5 seconds, the 11 recipes, which sources acid destroys, three small washed-up bags landing each day by world time alone, the tide taking them back after five days, and acid carrying them off, the loss card's text and length, and the cook ranking and lacking-first collection that AI and the NEXT line share. [Survival](../architecture/survival.md#temporary-content) lists the values.
- [Title screen #91](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/91) makes the game the only thing the application holds: it opens on a title screen offering a new game, the controls of both players, and quitting. The developer page, its experiments, and the earlier ascent prototype are removed.
- Temporary, not settled: the title screen is drawn with the screen's existing primitives over the monster mouth; its final art is not delivered.
- [Collection time #121](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/121) makes a collection hold only the actor who makes it while the day runs on real time, so both actors can collect at once and neither spends the other's day, and spreads the supply into small bags landing across the map every day from day 2 to day 50, so a human following NEXT spends most of a day walking the city. The rule is the same for a human and AI, and AI's share stays unlimited.
- The public contract and per-feature file ownership plan are in the [survival architecture](../architecture/survival.md). The UI, keys, and numbers in the concept images are not settled requirements. The originals the parent issue cites are in the [concept archive](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/tree/241124d5ba2dcef937c904623f1e5e3cb03fbcf5/docs/art/concept-art/monster-mouth-escape).

## Open questions

- The distinction between a base stay and actual cooking and the exploration clock, and detailed interaction when only one of the two actors is at base.
- Exploration time length, speed tuning, inventory size and weight limit, the slowdown over the limit, patrol stretches, and ingredient and recipe definitions.
- Acid units, the final rise and hold formulas, and the pace of map change over time.
- The final score threshold, the scope of repeated-food checks, next-day start conditions, the unfed penalty, how appraisal failure is expressed and its retry limits, and the final result of cooking failure.
- The causes and presentation of the endings other than "the monster is satisfied", and how the replaced escape chance after 30 days is retired in the code.
- Save and restore, distribution targets, and release conditions.
