Image processing: ImageMagick 7.1.1-43; Python standard library orchestrated subprocess calls. No PIL, external image service, public upload, or execution of generated data.
Each direct export uses its source_crop [x,y,width,height] from art_hd/manifest.json:
  magick SOURCE -crop WIDTHxHEIGHT+X+Y +repage -channel A -threshold 50% +channel -trim +repage CROP
After alpha threshold and trim, nearest sample to the entry's resize_pixels [rw,rh]. Actors use one common scale across all five frames of their species; no pose was resized independently. Each trimmed figure is centered horizontally on its canvas with its lowest opaque pixel immediately above baseline_y. The human fits within 150x170 and the rat within 184x96, beside the chef's 172px visible idle height.
Bottom-anchored sprites:
  magick -size CANVAS_WxCANVAS_H xc:none ( CROP -sample RWxRH! ) -geometry +LEFT+TOP -composite -depth 8 PNG32:OUTPUT
  LEFT=(CANVAS_W-RW)//2; TOP=baseline_y-RH.
Icons and direct UI exports:
  magick CROP -sample RWxRH! -background none -gravity center -extent CANVAS_WxCANVAS_H -depth 8 PNG32:OUTPUT
No palette quantization, blur, smooth scaling, hand-painted detail, or human retouch. The generated sheet pixels are the source of every exported picture.

First export inspection found the raised crate lid clipped at the canvas top and an adjacent weight-frame sliver inside the clock crop. The final export gives both crate states a 256x288 canvas and a 196x252 fit, and crops the clock source at [765,525,295,150]. Other source drawings were not regenerated.
