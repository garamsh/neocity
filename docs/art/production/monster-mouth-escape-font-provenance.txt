Grid: 1024 units per em, every outline point and advance a multiple of 128 units, so one of its pixels is an eighth of an em; capitals stand 5 of them. Its GSUB holds only ccmp and its GPOS only mark.
Runtime: project/levels/monster_mouth_escape/fonts/. Godot imports it with antialiasing, hinting and subpixel positioning off, set in the .import file beside it; the rest are Godot's defaults. rendering::Art draws it at whole multiples of 8 pixels only.
No glyph is edited and the font is not renamed. The licence's copyright line declares no Reserved Font Name.
Replaces Pixelify Sans, whose outlines are off a strict grid and whose antialiased C read as O at the interface's sizes in the review of PR #147.
