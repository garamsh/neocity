Monster-mouth escape asset provenance, 2026-11-02, issue #90

Version 2, after review of PR #96. Characters are cut from the approved concept sheets with no generation. Every other asset comes from Recraft, through its MCP connector, personal account. Every generation used the custom style below; Recraft ran it on model recraftv4_1_raster. No seed is exposed. Recraft returns WebP; the source URL of each generation is kept in tools/art/assets.json.
Tools: Python 3.13.5 runs tools/art/pixelize.py, standard library only. ImageMagick 7.1.1-43 Q16 x86_64 decodes WebP in pixelize.py fetch (PNG24, or PNG32 for background-removed sources).
Post-processing, the same for every asset, with per-asset values in assets.json; pixelize.py process:
1. Crops the rectangle given. A seamless tile cross-fades the last quarter of its crop into the first along each tiling axis and drops it, so its edges meet.
2. Masks the background: the given key colour connected to the border (or every pixel near it, for "enclosed"), or the alpha of a background-removed source; optionally keeps only the largest connected figure.
3. Applies the asset's recolour rules, if any: hue ranges moved to red (tomato, meat, full heart, vending machine) or desaturated to grey steel (UI frames, slot, plain button, clock case, empty heart, which is also lightened).
4. Detects the generation's own pixel grid from the spectrum of its column and row edges. Where both axes show a grid at least 4 times the median spectral strength, it moves the crop onto that grid so each output pixel is one drawn block; otherwise it divides the crop evenly. manifest.json does not record which; process prints it.
5. Takes one colour per block: the most frequent master-palette colour in the block's central half; a block is clear when most of it is background.
6. Limits the asset's colours to a subset of the 85-colour master palette in assets.json (64 median-cut from the three concept images, the 15 swatches of the two character sheets, and 6 greys from the concept's UI panels): 16 for characters, 12 for icons and UI, 24 for world tiles and props, 32 for backgrounds, workshop and key art, 16 for the logo. The most used colour is kept first, then each next pick maximises its use times its distance from the colours already kept, so small accents survive. Every other block takes its nearest kept colour.
7. Cleans: clears opaque pixels with at most one opaque 4-neighbour, fills clear pixels with three or more, and replaces a pixel that touches none of its own colour by the colour around it when the two are close, so contrasting single pixels such as eyes stay.
8. Characters and icons: sets every edge pixel to the outline colour #0a060e, giving a 1 px dark outline.
9. Enlarges by the asset's scale with nearest-neighbour. Every pixel is fully opaque or fully clear.
Raw generations are not committed. Running pixelize.py fetch and process again writes the committed PNGs and manifest.json byte for byte.

References: four crops of the concept images at commit 241124d5ba2dcef937c904623f1e5e3cb03fbcf5, docs/art/concept-art/monster-mouth-escape/: search-mockup-v1.png 380x230+1075+0 (fangs and throat), concept-v1.png 700x330+950+580 (acid sea and sunken cars), search-mockup-v1.png 700x330+0+290 (walkway, chef, appraiser), cook-feed-mockup-v1.png 1100x330+0+330 (kitchen).
Style prompt: Detailed 16-bit pixel art with crisp discrete pixel clusters, dark outlines and rich shading. Palette of pink and magenta flesh, cream fangs, indigo and violet buildings with warm orange windows, glowing neon green acid, rusty dark iron, warm amber lantern light.
