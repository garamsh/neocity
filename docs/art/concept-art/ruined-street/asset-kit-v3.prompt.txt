A production ASSET KIT SHEET on a plain dark neutral background, showing how the ruined cyberpunk street (attached round-2 concepts) is built from separate replaceable modular pieces, each drawn isolated with clear spacing like a sprite sheet, all in the same flat side-view pixel-art style and the same scale. Camera: strictly flat 2D side view, orthographic, like a classic side-scrolling platformer (Metal Slug, Dead Cells interiors). No perspective floors receding into depth, no three-quarter view, no vanishing points: floors are horizontal lines, walls face the camera, depth only by parallax layers behind. Rows: (1) facade wall tiles: intact concrete, cracked, blown-open with exposed rebar, burnt, acid-eaten; (2) walkable pieces: rooftop slab, broken rooftop edge, metal catwalk, bent catwalk, glass skybridge segment and a cracked one, helipad edge; (3) connectors: ladder, fire stair, service elevator cage, plank bridge; (4) props and containers: vending kiosk, parcel locker, fridge, crates, AC units, satellite dish, water tower; (5) signs: neon sign column lit and the same broken/dead, holo billboard lit and glitching; (6) debris and FX: rubble piles, hanging cables, sparks, smoke puff, acid drip. Small plain labels under each group row only (e.g. "FACADE", "WALKABLE", "CONNECTORS", "PROPS", "SIGNS", "DEBRIS"). No characters, no HUD.
