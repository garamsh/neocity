Each image must look exactly like a real 1920x1080 gameplay screenshot of the finished game: flat orthographic side view, gameplay camera, HUD on top, nothing painterly or cinematic, no text other than the HUD text given.

Use your built-in image generation tool. Attached references:

STYLE - NOT CUTE. The previous round of mockups was rejected for being cute. No chibi, no bright toy-like colours, no rounded clean cartoon props, no cosy look. Aim for a grim, grimy, mature ruined-cyberpunk horror atmosphere (think the detailed ruined backgrounds of Metal Slug, the mood of Blasphemous / Katana ZERO): a city swallowed inside a giant monster's mouth; wet flesh walls with veins, yellowed cracked teeth, dripping saliva and acid, rusted collapsed scaffolds, torn cables, broken flickering neon signs (Korean/English/kanji-like shapes, unreadable), smoke and acid fumes, debris, derailed train, dead vending machines, graffiti, grime. Dense, characterful ruins like the current game.

Fix what makes the current game look broken and tiring for the eyes, WITHOUT losing that grit:
1. ONE pixel grid for everything: as if the whole frame were drawn at 640x360 and scaled 3x nearest-neighbour; characters, tiles, props and background share the same pixel size. No fine 1px detail, no blur, no JPEG noise.
2. Eye comfort through value hierarchy, not by removing detail: the far background is darker, hazier and lower-contrast; the midground carries the detail; saturated neon and acid green are accents, not whole areas. No noisy high-frequency texture fighting everywhere.
3. Readability: walkable platforms have one consistent top edge and solid body and stand out from the background; the acid surface is one clear line.
4. Ingredients are real objects in the world (vending machine, crate, shelf, washed-up bag) with only a tiny count badge; only the nearest interactable gets a subtle thin outline glow. NO dark square icon plates, NO yellow square frames, NO dashed boxes.
5. Characters about about 150 px tall on screen, standing on platforms, with a contact shadow.
