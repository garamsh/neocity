## Status and source

- Status: AI-authored candidates; version 1. These are imagined gameplay compositions, not runtime screenshots, adopted canon, production assets, or validated level layouts.
- Source: the user–AI conversation on 2026-10-30 (Asia/Seoul), recorded in [issue #19's scope extension](https://github.com/garamsh/neocity/issues/19#issuecomment-6515011779). No external discussion authorship is claimed.
- Generating agent: Codex acting as PM; the Art Worker copied and inspected the seven supplied outputs and assembled the exhibit. Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, one call per image, `transparent_background: false`.
- Model, seed, quality setting, and exact call start/completion times were not supplied to the Art Worker. The output modification times below were read from the actual PNG files and are not claimed as model execution times.
- PNG dimensions and byte sizes were read from the files. All outputs were copied byte-for-byte without image editing. The original local paths identify the session; the relative image links are portable.
- Every icon, gauge, heart counter, background sign, displayed population count, and machine shape is illustrative. Written rules and minimum prototype sizes are proposals to test, not behavior established by the images.
